# CertivoIQ PHA Role Commercial Campaign

## Production standard

- Six live-action commercials with real professionals in authentic housing-agency, office, meeting, and audit-preparation environments.
- No cartoons, animated people, synthetic presenters, exaggerated acting, or robotic voices.
- Motion comes from camera movement, people working, document handling, monitor interaction, meetings, and restrained interface/text animation.
- Brand palette: CertivoIQ navy, teal, white, and restrained gold accents.
- Master line: **Processed by your software. Independently reviewed by CertivoIQ.**
- Brand line: **Stay Audit Ready with CertivoIQ.**
- Voice pronunciation: Certivo, /ˈsɜrɾəˌvo/.
- Platform references may be spoken and rendered as plain text: Yardi Voyager PHA, Emphasys, MRI PHA Pro, or the agency's existing housing-management platform. Do not use third-party logos without permission.
- Never claim that CertivoIQ guarantees compliance, eliminates findings, or replaces agency judgment.
- Music: modern, restrained enterprise pulse; confident rather than dramatic.
- Accessibility: burned-in captions plus a clean caption file; never rely on audio alone.

## Silent-safe supers

Every commercial must communicate the complete message with audio muted:

1. Role headline
2. “Processed by your software.”
3. “Independently reviewed by CertivoIQ.”
4. Two or three role-specific proof points
5. Role-specific CTA
6. `certivoiq.com/pha/{persona}`
7. “Stay Audit Ready with CertivoIQ.”

## Production QA gates

- Real adult humans in every people scene; no illustrations or avatar presenters.
- Natural hands, screens, office behavior, facial expressions, and movement.
- No screen claims that exceed currently implemented CertivoIQ capabilities.
- No unauthorised platform logos or copied proprietary UI.
- CertivoIQ wordmark spelling is exact in every frame.
- Voiceover is natural, human-sounding, and correctly pronounces Certivo.
- CTA and destination match the persona.
- Captions match the final voiceover exactly.
- Final exports are reviewed at full speed and frame-by-frame for text errors, visual artifacts, and role mismatch.
