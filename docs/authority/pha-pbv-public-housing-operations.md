# PHA PBV and Public Housing operations authority

Validated against eCFR Title 24 current through August 25, 2026.

## PBV
- 24 CFR part 983 governs PBV.
- 24 CFR 983.2 excludes voucher portability rules from PBV and applies PBV-specific provisions instead.
- 24 CFR 983.261 governs family right to move after one year of PBV assistance, with VAWA emergency-transfer handling separately preserved.
- 24 CFR 983.301-.305 govern rent to owner, reasonableness, caps, redeterminations, and written notice.

## Public Housing
- 24 CFR 960.253 governs choice of rent and annual rent-option administration.
- 24 CFR 960.507 governs Public Housing over-income families, including the 24-consecutive-month threshold and PHA policy choices after that threshold.

CertivoIQ keeps PHA policy choices source-gated and does not infer local Administrative Plan or ACOP decisions.
