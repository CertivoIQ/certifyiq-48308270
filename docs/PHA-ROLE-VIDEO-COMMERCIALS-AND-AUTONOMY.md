# CertivoIQ PHA Role-Specific Commercial Campaign & Autonomy Plan

## Campaign positioning

**Processed by your software. Independently reviewed by CertivoIQ.**

CertivoIQ is positioned as an independent compliance-review layer that works alongside Yardi Voyager PHA, Emphasys, MRI PHA Pro, and other housing-management systems. It is not positioned as a replacement for those systems and does not claim to guarantee compliance or eliminate audit findings.

Primary brand line: **Stay Audit Ready with CertivoIQ**

- `/pha/finance-operations` — CFO / COO
- `/pha/technology` — CIO / IT Director

Every ad must deep-link directly to the matching role page. Preserve UTM parameters for source, medium, campaign, creative, and role.

## Commercial production rules

- Realistic human actors only; no cartoon, animated, synthetic-looking, or game-style people.
- Premium enterprise visual language: dark navy, white, teal accents, CertivoIQ gold.
- Use motion throughout: camera movement, office activity, UI zooms, files being reviewed, dashboards updating, and restrained kinetic typography.
- Avoid robotic voices. Voiceover should sound natural, authoritative, calm, and human.
- Certivo pronunciation cue for voice talent: `/ˈsɜrɾəˌvo/`.
- Do not use language such as “ensures compliance,” “validates compliance,” or “eliminates audit findings.”
- Prefer “independently reviews,” “identifies compliance risk,” “surfaces missing and conflicting evidence,” “supports consistent certification review,” and “helps teams correct issues before audit.”

## Master 30-second commercial structure

### 0–4 sec — The pressure
Real leasing / PHA office. Staff member at a desk with certification files, dual monitors, and a visible queue of work. Slow camera push-in.

On-screen: **Your software processed the certification.**

### 4–9 sec — The blind spot
Close shots of income calculations, missing documents, conflicting values, and reviewer notes. The employee looks concerned but professional, not melodramatic.

Voiceover: “But processing a certification is not the same as independently reviewing the compliance risk.”

### 9–16 sec — CertivoIQ enters
Smooth transition into a realistic CertivoIQ interface. Findings appear with linked evidence, source documents, rule versions, and unresolved exceptions.

On-screen: **Independently reviewed by CertivoIQ.**

Voiceover: “CertivoIQ reviews completed affordable-housing certifications for calculation issues, missing documentation, inconsistent information, and compliance risk.”

### 16–23 sec — Role-specific value
Swap this scene by audience. Show the exact dashboard/workflow relevant to the decision-maker.

### 23–27 sec — Existing system remains
Show a split workflow: housing-management system on one screen, CertivoIQ review layer on the other.

Voiceover: “Keep your existing housing-management system. Add an independent compliance-review layer.”

### 27–30 sec — CTA
CertivoIQ logo, gold IQ/checkmark, clean dark navy background with subtle moving UI fragments.

On-screen: **Stay Audit Ready with CertivoIQ**

Secondary line: **Processed by your software. Independently reviewed by CertivoIQ.**

CTA varies by role.

# Autonomous marketing and sales operating model

The owner should handle escalations, not routine operations.

## Autonomous loop

1. **Traffic attribution**
   - Every ad carries role, platform, campaign, creative, and source UTMs.
   - Visitor lands directly on the matching role page.

2. **Lead capture**
   - Role is prefilled/hidden from the route.
   - Capture agency, contact, title, email, phone, current platform, units, consent, and campaign attribution.
   - Reject obvious spam and non-business submissions.

3. **CRM routing**
   - Automatically create/update account and contact.
   - Assign persona, source, campaign, funnel stage, and next-best action.
   - Duplicate leads are merged rather than creating parallel records.

4. **Automated follow-up**
   - Send the role-specific confirmation immediately.
   - Send role-specific proof/workflow content next.
   - If the prospect engages, move to demo-ready status.
   - If the prospect does not engage, enter a finite nurture sequence; do not spam indefinitely.

5. **Demo and sales qualification**
   - Route the request into the correct demonstration experience.
   - Collect current system, program mix, portfolio size, timing, and decision process before a human sales conversation where possible.

6. **Invoice / enterprise purchasing**
   - Enterprise and multifamily/PHA purchases use invoice-led sales rather than forcing card checkout.
   - Routine invoice preparation, CRM recording, payment-status updates, and customer notices should be automated.
   - Escalate only exceptions such as unusual legal terms, pricing exceptions, failed payment, disputed invoices, or non-standard procurement requirements.

7. **Onboarding**
   - Self-directed onboarding collects organization, program, property/agency, user, system, and authority configuration.
   - Require customer verification of property-specific figures and project/local overlays before those values become authoritative.

8. **SupportIQ**
   - Routine questions are answered automatically using approved product/support knowledge.
   - Automatically resolve low-risk operational requests where authority exists.
   - Escalate billing disputes, legal questions, security incidents, account ownership conflicts, policy exceptions, and unresolved technical failures.

9. **Compliance operations**
   - CertivoIQ may automatically extract, evaluate, flag, classify, route, and prepare findings.
   - It must not silently resolve uncertain/conflicting compliance evidence.
   - High-risk, conflicting, unsupported, or authority-sensitive cases become **Agent Action Required** and enter Manual Review / Verification / Approval / Signature as applicable.

10. **Marketing operations**
    - Track CTR, landing-page conversion, demo conversion, qualified-pipeline conversion, CAC, and role-level performance.
    - Pause underperforming creatives only within pre-approved rules and budget boundaries.
    - Never increase spend, create a new paid channel, or exceed an approved budget without owner approval.

## Owner escalation queue

Only surface items requiring judgment, authority, or financial approval:

- Pricing exception beyond configured threshold
- Contract/legal redline
- Security or privacy incident
- Compliance rule/source conflict requiring authoritative human decision
- Customer dispute or executive complaint
- Failed enterprise payment requiring judgment
- Non-standard procurement condition
- High-value opportunity requiring executive involvement
- Paid-media budget increase or new paid resource
- Product decision with material legal, financial, or reputational impact

## Daily owner digest

One concise digest should summarize:

- New qualified leads
- Demos requested/booked
- Enterprise invoices issued / paid / overdue
- New customers and onboarding status
- Support escalations
- Compliance escalations requiring owner action
- Marketing performance anomalies
- Security/operations incidents

If there are no escalations, the owner should not be asked to perform routine work.
