# CertivoIQ Low-Cost Autopilot GTM and Support Plan

## Operating objective

Build a digital-first sales and customer-success motion that minimizes founder phone time, uses automation for routine education and qualification, and escalates humans only for consequential issues.

## Funnel

1. **Attract** - compliance methodology pages, regulatory explainers, audit-readiness checklists, webinars recorded once and reused, and targeted professional posts.
2. **Capture** - downloadable audit-readiness checklist or self-assessment in exchange for business email and company/property information.
3. **Qualify** - automated form scoring based on portfolio size, programs, certification volume, audit timing, current systems, and buying authority.
4. **Nurture** - automated email sequence with evidence-based use cases, product videos, methodology, security material, and ROI calculator.
5. **Demo without calls** - interactive product tour, short recorded demos, sandbox/demo account, FAQ, and procurement/security package.
6. **Human escalation** - only high-value enterprise procurement, security review, integration scoping, billing disputes, critical UI blockers, or explicitly requested executive conversations.
7. **Onboard** - in-product checklist, guided setup, knowledge base, virtual agent, and automated lifecycle email.
8. **Expand** - usage-triggered prompts for additional properties, portfolios, integrations, agency workflows, and enterprise governance.

## Founder escalation policy

Founder phone involvement should normally be limited to:

- material billing/payment disputes;
- severe production or UI incidents affecting customer operations;
- enterprise security/procurement decisions requiring an executive;
- strategic contracts above an internally defined approval threshold;
- legal/regulatory matters that require counsel or executive action.

Everything else should default to asynchronous email, help center, product messaging, ticketing, or a virtual support agent.

## Virtual customer service agent

The virtual agent should be retrieval-grounded on approved CertivoIQ documentation and permitted account data. It should:

- answer product and onboarding questions;
- explain interface workflows;
- retrieve approved compliance methodology without pretending to be legal counsel;
- collect bug reports and reproduction steps;
- triage billing questions without exposing sensitive payment data;
- create structured escalation summaries;
- refuse to make unauthorized certification or regulatory decisions;
- escalate when confidence is low or the issue is consequential.

Recommended operating principle: automate the first response and information gathering, not irreversible decisions.

## Near-zero cash acquisition channels

- Founder/expert LinkedIn content produced in batches and scheduled.
- SEO pages around affordable-housing compliance questions and audit preparation.
- Free audit-readiness self-assessment.
- Referral partnerships with affordable-housing consultants and trainers.
- Commission-based independent sales partners for closed, collected business, subject to written agreements and applicable law.
- Customer referral credits or service credits.
- Recorded educational webinars reused as evergreen lead magnets.
- Email nurture to opt-in prospects and legitimate business contacts with required compliance controls.

A literal $0 growth system is not realistic at scale: email delivery, CRM, AI inference, hosting, payment processing, legal review, and support infrastructure eventually create costs. The practical goal is **near-zero fixed acquisition payroll with variable costs tied to usage or closed revenue**.

## Commission model concept

For independent referral/sales partners, consider a written plan such as a percentage of first-year collected subscription revenue, paid only after customer payment and subject to clawback for early cancellation. Do not use commission-only arrangements as a substitute for reviewing worker-classification, wage, tax, licensing, anti-kickback, or procurement rules that may apply to the specific seller or customer.

## Automation KPIs

- percentage of leads qualified without human touch;
- percentage of demos completed asynchronously;
- lead-to-paid conversion;
- time to first value;
- support deflection rate;
- escalation rate;
- customer acquisition cost;
- gross margin;
- expansion revenue;
- churn and retention.
