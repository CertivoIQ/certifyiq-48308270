# CertivoIQ Zero-Cost Marketing System

## Objective

Create qualified demand without paid ads, cold calling, or a large sales team. The primary conversion event is **3 FREE certification reviews** with company information captured before upload.

## Core funnel

```text
Useful content / direct outreach / referrals
        ↓
certivoiq.com/welcome
        ↓
Review Your 3 FREE Certifications
        ↓
Company + decision-maker capture
        ↓
3 certification reviews
        ↓
Findings + evidence + human approval
        ↓
Plan recommendation
        ↓
Stripe subscription
```

## Channel 1 — LinkedIn organic

Daily, publish one useful post aimed at owners, operators, compliance leaders, and property-management executives.

Content pillars:

1. **Compliance risk:** explain one common certification failure and how to catch it.
2. **HOTMA:** explain one practical income/asset issue at a time.
3. **Audit readiness:** show what a reviewer should verify before an auditor arrives.
4. **Evidence:** demonstrate why traceable source evidence matters.
5. **Product proof:** anonymized screenshots/workflows showing how CertivoIQ turns a file into findings.
6. **Founder/operator perspective:** lessons from building compliance infrastructure.

Every post gets one CTA:

> **Run 3 FREE certification reviews at CertivoIQ.com.**

Do not use multiple CTAs in the same post.

## Channel 2 — Permission-based email

Use the existing cold-intro template only for relevant affordable-housing organizations and follow applicable email/anti-spam requirements.

Sequence:

- Email 1: identify a specific compliance problem + invite the 3 FREE reviews.
- Email 2: share one practical compliance insight + reminder of the free reviews.
- Email 3: show the evidence/human-approval workflow + invite them to test one real file.
- Email 4: final low-pressure reminder + pricing link.

Do not use phone calls as the default CTA. The preferred CTA is the website/free-review workflow.

## Channel 3 — Free educational assets

Create one downloadable asset per week from existing expertise:

- HOTMA certification review checklist
- LIHTC pre-audit checklist
- Section 8 file-review checklist
- Affordable-housing certification evidence checklist
- 2026 compliance review worksheet

Each asset should have one landing CTA to the 3 FREE certification reviews.

## Channel 4 — Communities and professional conversations

Participate where affordable-housing professionals already discuss compliance. Answer questions first; do not spam links. When a question directly relates to certification review, evidence, or audit readiness, offer the free-review workflow as the practical next step.

## Channel 5 — Referral loop

Every completed free review should produce a simple referral prompt:

> “Know another owner or compliance leader who reviews certifications? Send them the 3 FREE review link.”

For paid customers, create a customer referral program later. Do not build paid referral incentives before launch unless there is a clear economic case.

## Channel 6 — SEO from product knowledge

Turn every recurring compliance question into a permanent article.

Recommended first topics:

- What should be checked before a LIHTC certification is approved?
- HOTMA income and asset review checklist
- Affordable housing certification audit checklist
- Common Section 8 certification errors
- How to build an audit-ready certification file
- What evidence should support a certification finding?

Articles should link to the 3 FREE review CTA and relevant product methodology pages.

## Daily zero-cost operating rhythm

### 60–90 minutes

**20 min — Publish**

Post one useful LinkedIn insight.

**20 min — Engage**

Leave thoughtful comments on 10 relevant industry posts.

**20 min — Outreach**

Send 10–20 personalized, permission-aware emails or LinkedIn messages to highly relevant prospects. Do not use a generic blast.

**10 min — Follow-up**

Respond to free-review leads and people who engaged with prior content.

**10 min — Product proof**

Capture one screenshot, finding example, workflow explanation, or short educational clip for future content.

Track only the numbers that move the funnel:

- Qualified prospects contacted
- Landing-page visits
- Free-review starts
- Company profiles completed
- Certification #1 completed
- Certification #2 completed
- Certification #3 completed
- Pricing-page visits
- Stripe checkouts started
- Paid subscriptions
- Free-review → paid conversion rate
- Referral leads

## Launch-week target

Do not optimize for followers. Optimize for qualified product usage.

A useful first-week target is:

- 100 highly relevant prospects reached
- 25+ free-review starts
- 10+ first certifications reviewed
- 5+ users reaching certification #3
- 2+ paid conversions

These are operating targets, not guarantees.

## Messaging rule

Keep the same core promise everywhere:

> **CertivoIQ finds compliance risk before the auditor does.**
>
> **AI does the reading. Your team owns the decision.**
>
> **Start with 3 FREE certification reviews — no card required.**

Avoid claims that imply autonomous legal approval, guaranteed savings, guaranteed audit outcomes, or guaranteed avoidance of penalties.

## No-cost tools

Use tools already available before adding subscriptions:

- CertivoIQ website/free-review funnel
- LinkedIn organic publishing and direct messaging
- Existing email capability
- GitHub for public technical/product credibility where appropriate
- Google Search Console and free analytics where available
- Spreadsheet/CRM tracking already present in CertivoIQ
- Existing screen-recording/video capabilities for short educational demonstrations

The rule is simple: **prove the funnel organically before spending on advertising.**
