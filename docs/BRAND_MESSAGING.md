# CertivoIQ Brand Messaging

## Positioning
**CertivoIQ — The compliance intelligence infrastructure for affordable housing programs.**

## Primary value proposition
**Find compliance risk before the auditor.**

CertivoIQ turns certification data, federal compliance requirements, versioned rules, and supporting evidence into actionable compliance intelligence for affordable housing owners, operators, property managers, and compliance teams.

## Customer-facing language
Lead with compliance intelligence, federal baseline review, evidence, requirements, traceability, risk identification, visibility, workflow, and audit readiness.

Do not position CertivoIQ publicly as an AI product. Avoid customer-facing phrases such as "AI-powered," "AI analyst," "AI copilot," or "AI document processing." Underlying implementation technology is not the customer-facing category or brand identity.

## Current review scope
CertivoIQ currently provides a **Federal Baseline Compliance Review** using supported, versioned federal affordable-housing requirements.

Do not claim complete state, HFA, allocating-agency, local, or project-specific compliance. Do not advertise nationwide state-specific coverage or sell state rule packs. If an unevaluated jurisdictional or project-specific requirement could change the result, the review must return **Unable to Determine** and identify the limitation.

Approved scope statement:

> CertivoIQ evaluates certification evidence against supported federal affordable-housing requirements using versioned, traceable rules. State-agency, allocating-agency, local, and project-specific requirements require separate manual review.

## Agent governance terminology
In CertivoIQ materials, **agent** means an authorized compliance agent acting for the customer. It never means CertivoIQ or an automated software agent.

- **Manual Review** — a person examines evidence or findings outside automated evaluation.
- **Agent Verification** — an authorized compliance agent confirms that a value matches its source evidence.
- **Agent Action Required** — processing is paused until an authorized compliance agent resolves a conflict, supplies information, or makes a permitted decision.
- **Agent Approval** — an authorized compliance agent accepts, rejects, or returns the overall review.
- **Agent Signature** — the named agent formally signs the completed review.
- **Agent-Controlled Review** — the authorized compliance agent controls resolution, approval, and completion.

The first public reference should use **authorized compliance agent**. Shorter interface labels may use **Agent Approval**, **Agent Signature**, and **Agent Action Required** after the role has been defined.

## Compliance claims
Do not claim or imply guaranteed avoidance of penalties, fines, tax-credit loss, HUD findings, regulatory action, audit findings, enforcement, or violations. Do not present CertivoIQ as legal advice, complete jurisdictional approval, or a guarantee that an audit will have no findings.

## Pricing
CertivoIQ has two operating-platform licenses: **Multifamily Enterprise is $65,000 per selected state per year**, and **PHA is $150,000 per organization per year**. **Merlin is an optional organization-wide compliance-intelligence add-on at $5,000 per month under a 12-month agreement ($60,000 annually)** and includes up to 50,000 certification or recertification analyses per month across licensed states and programs. Standard overage is $0.15 per additional certification. An approved month-to-month Merlin arrangement is $6,000 per month. Standard implementation is **$15,000 one time per organization**.

Multifamily messaging should emphasize property and portfolio certifications, licensed-state rule guides, evidence reconciliation, deterministic outcomes, exception visibility, and pending final review controls. PHA messaging should emphasize HCV, PBV, public housing, Mod Rehab, HUD-50058, waiting lists, portability, lease-up, admissions, occupancy, HOTMA, NSPIRE, accommodations, notices, policies, agency users, and agency-wide reporting.

Position Merlin as research, explanation, inconsistency analysis, exception prioritization, regulatory-change monitoring, and audit-response assistance. Never claim Merlin overrides the rule engine or approves a finding. Final confirmation requires an authorized responsible party's signature and position.

Do not publish Academy or training products, certificates, or separate API prices. The primary sales action is **Try CertivoIQ for Free**.
