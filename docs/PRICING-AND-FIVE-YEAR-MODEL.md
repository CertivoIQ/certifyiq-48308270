# CertivoIQ Pricing Strategy and Five-Year Planning Model

## Pricing principle

CertivoIQ should be priced against enterprise value, compliance workflow scope, portfolio size, certification volume, implementation complexity, integrations, security requirements, and support obligations - not simply against hosting cost.

A potential $65,000 tax-credit takeback or other compliance loss can be useful context for value discussions, but it must not be represented as a guaranteed loss CertivoIQ will prevent. Enterprise buyers also value labor savings, faster review, audit readiness, consistency, traceability, and portfolio visibility.

## Recommended commercial architecture

Avoid making $80,000/month the default Enterprise list price at the current stage. $960,000 annual recurring revenue from one customer can be realistic only for a very large strategic deployment with substantial scope, portfolio volume, integrations, implementation, governance, security commitments, and measurable enterprise value.

A stronger path is tiered enterprise pricing:

- **Growth / Portfolio:** approximately $60k-$120k ARR depending on volume and scope.
- **Enterprise:** approximately $150k-$300k ARR with larger portfolios, advanced governance, reporting, and support.
- **Enterprise Plus / Strategic:** approximately $300k-$600k+ ARR for major portfolios, agency workflows, advanced integrations, dedicated environments/support, or high certification volume.
- **Strategic national agreement:** $600k-$1M+ ARR only when deployment scope and proven value justify it.

Implementation, migration, custom integration, and premium support may be priced separately rather than hidden inside subscription ARR.

## Pricing validation milestones

Before moving the standard Enterprise plan toward $80k/month, establish evidence for:

1. repeatable production outcomes across multiple paying customers;
2. documented time/cost reduction and compliance-quality improvements;
3. enterprise security and procurement readiness;
4. reliable integrations and implementation methodology;
5. strong retention and expansion;
6. willingness-to-pay interviews with economic buyers;
7. referenceable customers;
8. a defined strategic deployment whose value supports a near-seven-figure annual contract.

## ROI framing

Preferred framing:

**Annual platform value = labor capacity recovered + avoided rework + faster audit preparation + reduced external consulting burden + portfolio visibility + documented risk reduction.**

Use potential recapture, penalties, or findings as risk context only when factually supported for the customer's specific program and circumstances. Do not promise that CertivoIQ eliminates or guarantees avoidance of recapture, fines, findings, or noncompliance.
